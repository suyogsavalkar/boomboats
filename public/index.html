HOW TO PLAY
  1. Drive with W A S D or the arrow keysthe joystick (bottom left).
  2. Grab a weapon by driving through one. The coloured circle shows how strong it is, and the yellow arrow points to the nearest one.
  3. Aim at a boat: a red target means your shot will hit.
  4. Fire with SPACE or a clickthe FIRE button (bottom right). One tap fires the whole weapon, so keep pointing at your target.
  5. Hide behind rocks and walls. They block every shot.
  6. Sunk? Tap REVIVE after 5 seconds. You get 4 seconds of protection.
  7. Most boats sunk in 3 minutes wins.
