Boomboats - audio credits
=========================

All sounds below are real recordings (or edits of real recordings), trimmed,
mixed to mono, loudness-matched and re-encoded (.ogg Vorbis + .mp3) for the game.
Loops were made seamless with a short equal-power crossfade of the recording onto itself.
(The "kenney/" subfolder was already here and is not covered by this file.)

           https://archive.org/details/aporee_57817_66187
  Author:  kaoss012
  License: Public Domain Mark 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/)
  Edit:    45.0-50.5 s section, 35 Hz high-pass, looped
